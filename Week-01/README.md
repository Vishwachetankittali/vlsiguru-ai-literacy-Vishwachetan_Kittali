## Reflection

### What I understood
AI is not the same as ordinary automation: rule-based programs follow
instructions a person wrote, while machine learning finds patterns in
data. Language models generate text one token at a time by predicting the
next token, which is why they can sound fluent and still be wrong. My
multiplication test showed this: ChatGPT's steps were correct but its
final total was off by 72,000. I also learned that agents are systems
built around a model with tools and a loop, and that important claims
need a primary or authoritative source, not just an AI answer.

### What still confuses me
- How an AI assistant decides when to use a tool such as Python, and how reliable that is.

### One thing I will verify differently next time
I will check numbers and facts with a tool or primary source instead of
trusting a neat explanation, and I will pick test questions that I can
verify quickly. I will also log each check as I go instead of at the end.
